Leaf Detectives

Help Pip and a lost golden leaf find their tree. Discover why leaves change color, check an AI guess with real clues, and turn fallen leaves into art.

Download workbook ↓Watch the videos

PDF · 5 pages · US Letter · Print at actual size

A LITTLE PREPARATION

Make room for discovery.

Bring the printed workbook, fallen leaves, paper, crayons, a glue stick, and child-safe scissors. A grown-up leads the AI photo activity and supervises collecting and making. Do not touch or taste unknown plants.

Read our grown-up guidance ↗

What we’ll explore

  • Explain why leaves look green and why some change color in fall.
  • Use leaf and plant photos to ask AI and check its guess.
  • Identify the leaf shape and vein clues AI can compare in a photo.

Pip: Page 4! You checked AI’s guess. Now be the maker!

Narrator: Playing and building with your hands is one of the best ways to learn.

Narrator: Leaf Rubbing! Make a hidden leaf pattern appear by rubbing a crayon over paper.

Narrator: You need: one fallen leaf, one sheet of paper, and one crayon with the wrapper peeled off.

Narrator: Step 1: Put the leaf under the paper. The bumpy side with the veins should face up.

Narrator: Step 2: Hold the paper still. Rub gently with the side of the crayon.

Narrator: Step 3: Keep rubbing. Watch the leaf’s outline and veins appear!

Pip: Whoa, it’s like magic!

Narrator: Leaf Creature! Collect more fallen leaves. Then make a leaf creature!

Narrator: You need: fallen leaves, two sheets of paper, child-safe scissors, a glue stick, and crayons or markers.

Narrator: Step 1: Lay the paper flat. Arrange the leaves on the paper until you like your creature.

Narrator: Step 2: Lift one leaf at a time. Rub glue on its back, then press it onto the paper.

Narrator: Step 3: Draw eyes, a beak, and legs on the second sheet. Cut them out, then glue them onto the leaves.

Pip: Hoo-hoo! What creature will you make? Pause and make yours!
